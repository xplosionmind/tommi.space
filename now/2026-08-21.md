It’s been almost one year since the last time I updated this page. Meanwhile, many big changes happened both inside and outside of me, definitely more than I could have documented here, maybe even more than what I was able to process myself.

Now, either I get used to write more frequent and concise updates, or I have to give up to the inevitable incommunicability of the kineticism of my existence.

Anyway, here is what I am dedicating my time to lately.

First off, it’s the first time in years that I spend **the whole August** in West Liguria, without interruption. It’s quite impactful on me, especially considering my proposition of finding a place where I want to set roots—see <q>Propositions</q> below.

## 🌻 Resisting in West Liguria, with Scambi Festival

I am so glad and grateful for the direction we are taking at [Scambi Festival](https://scambi.org/en/). This year’s edition is the fifth, it’s themed **<q>Resistance</q>**, and we are organising it with the smallest team so far. We are less than 10 under-30 volunteers, and we are most fundamentally a group of friends who care about each other.

We are still one of the very few youth organisations in West Liguria, but I am very excited because in the last couple of years other associations were born around us, and we are slowly building a local network. **A network of Ligurian resistance**.

From August 26<sup>th</sup> to 30<sup>th</sup>, we are hosting a **summer school** for the second time. Finally, on Saturday 29<sup>th</sup> August, [**Queen of Saba**](https://www.instagram.com/queenofsaba.band/ '@queenofsaba.band on Instagram') are coming to play live! We have been in touch for at least two years to welcome them in Sanremo, and the time is here, at last. I am psyched because they are the coolest and hottest artists in Italy right now, but I am also terrified because it’s the first time we are setting up a stage, and it’s a huge responsibility for me as the president of the association. Aaaaaanyway, it’s all going to be GREAT.

Even though theoretically we are in the summer break, I am reflecting a lot on the path I want to take with my research at [XPUB](https://xpub.nl), especially as I have a dissertation to write and a project to develop, to eventually **graduate in July 2027**.

## 🧑‍💻 Unlocking the next level of Linux System Administration

In the last five months I improved my Linux System Administration skills more than in the past five years.

Even though I am immensely grateful to [YunoHost](/yunohost/) for allowing me to self-host super easily, [I felt the need to abandon it](/yunohost/quit/) in favour of a different infrastructure that would allow me to have more understanding and control.

After years of longing, I finally configured [Bocia](/bocia/) with [Immich](https://immich.app) in the living room of [my Rotterdam home](https://fatima.house/), and I am now using [Podman](/podman/) to install, configure, and maintain all the services I host.

I am also quite satisfied for becoming a biiit better at documenting my findings and the configuration steps for my infrastructure.

I still have much more to learn, but I think I unlocked a new skillset level. I am ready to get to the next ones, now.
