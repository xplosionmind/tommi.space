Here are my notes, ideas, and the bibliography of <cite>[A Jester’s Guide to the Fediverse](https://ctalx.c-base.org/fediday-2026/talk/WMCETA/)</cite>, my talk at [Berlin FediDay 2026](https://berlinfedi.day/en/).

## Notes

- Going where the people are cool but techno-averse (not techno music but technology) make them **curious** first. Put them in the position where *they are asking you* to join the Fediverse.
	- stickers
	- tattoo
	- clown and jester things
- Neighborhoods and **proximity**
- Motivational skelrtor meme
- vulgarising the concept of standards as languages instead of platforms as land
- Seize the means of communication → proximity computing
- <q>Stay hungry, stay fool-ish</q> — Steve Jollys
- Jester apparently cog in a machine, but working on a longer term timeline, changing the world in an invisible yet prolonged way
- not only being a bridge horizontally, connecting different communities. Not only being a bridge vertically, connecting the hyper local with the global, the universal. Most importantly, connecting **diagonally**, being a bridge in different fields, with different approaches, completely shifting the thinking paradox.

## Bard notes

- Spreading all over Europe because exiled by Roman Empire
- Independent of gender
- Any sort of art, but especially musicians
- Unlike any other medieval profession, they come from every social class
- Jester: Delivering the news, gossip
- Need to improvise and adapt
- Need to be under the spotlight when called upon, but also to fade in the background when needed
- Free rein to say whatever they wanted
- Not merely entertainment, but their practice as a way to ease the daily life of court, lightening stressful matters.
- <q>Jester’s privilege</q>: being able to tease their patron with uncomfortable truth.
- Court was full of lies and subterfuge: the jester was frank and masterfully truthful. (FediJester in the era of post-truth)
- Joyful outfit and coloured, lack of symmetry, unpredictability. Heard tinkling everywhere they went. (FediJester and the FediCape)
- Endurance of continuous entertainment during festivities (for several days)
