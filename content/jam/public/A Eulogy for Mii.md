Mii were born on <time datetime=2026-11-19>19 November 2006</time>. Even though they still theoretically exist, we declared them dead as Nintendo discontinued StreetPass, on <time datetime=2024-04-08>8<sup>th</sup> April 2024</time>.

This eulogy is made of two parts, one written by me, and one written by [Holly](https://holly.garden/ 'Holly’s personal website'). We recided it alternately as the other hummed the Mii Channel – Plaza Theme.

https://youtu.be/eqtZPO7XSKk

> You were so round and shiny, so goofy yet very put together. Since the first moment I met you, I knew I would have spent many hours in your company. When you met my friends, your presence mad our time together even better.
>
> At times, I just found myself staring at you, walking around, glowing with the light, cheerful, ephemeral attitude that made you the most wonderful and relatable digital artefact I have ever known.
>
> We shared adventures, and, when times where boring, I just made more of you, moulded after my mum, my grandpa, my great-great aunt, my unborn cousins: my whole family and the one I wish I had turned around you. Thank you Mii.
