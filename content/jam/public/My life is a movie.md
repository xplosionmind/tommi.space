Since I started going to therapy (and probably even before then, but not consciously), I have been using a very effective metaphor to describe the recurrent, uncontrollable, and sudden change in perspective in my life.

Sometimes, briefly yet not too rarely, I see my life as a movie of which **I am the main character**. Whatever happens, good or bad, <q>it makes up for the plot</q>—cit. [Alice Bleasdale](https://instagram.com/alice_bleasdale '@alice_bleasdale on Instagram'). It is part of the story, part of the game. Tragedies occur, joyful moments arrive, and it is how life works. Living life as if it was a movie does not have to imply taking it lightly or not being enraged by what is wrong with the world (or hating billionaires, or destroying fascists), it is about *contextualising* it. It is about shining the right light on it. Exactly as a movie, it needs the right cinematography. It cannot be too dark, otherwise you understand nothing, like [in any <cite>Game of Thrones</cite> battle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUtbK8wX5nQ). It cannot be [<em lang=it>smarmellato</em>](https://youtu.be/WbopcNPmt34) or it becomes cheesy and fake.

https://youtu.be/4cc9jyFMD5Q

Nevertheless, most of the time my sensitivity, my empathy, and my overall way of being make me feel everything, anything, much more intensely. Every single thought or event acquires a wholesome, totalising weight, and my positivity is swallowed by the overbearing weight of the current situation. Even though rationally I try to perceive my life as a movie, I cannot feel it at all. I am stuck in the vortex of current events and emotions, and I fail to see the bigger picture.

For this reason, I admire and I do my best to be inspired by fictional characters and real friends who I believe are great at living their life as if it was a movie.

The best of the best of the best at this is none other than Fleabag ❤️

https://youtu.be/b8pDI0dC2UM

## Crossovers

If life is a movie, then the exsistence of a network of people is a magnificent set of crossovers. Zooming out even more, society would become the universe’s most breathtaking TV series, or franchise, or saga.

As I briefly and chaotically mentioned in my interview at FiresideFedi, it is even more amazing to observe how a crossover in my life is actually a cameo in another person’s exsistance, and this is incredibly fascinating.
