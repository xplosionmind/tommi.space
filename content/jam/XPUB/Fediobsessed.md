## Background and context

Unsurprisingly, <cite>Fediobsessed</cite> originally sparked out of a toot.

The toot itself mentions a random and spontaneous event that made me realise  that I am not the right person to host a show *about* the Fediverse, but I can definitely host one where I obsess over it, convincing someone else to join the fun.

I lost the toot so I am embedding another toot that is what I think about when I forget toots:

https://mastodon.social/@admiralwonderboat/116602426551386568

This radio show definitely works as a standalone project, but it perfectly complements and integrates <cite>[The Art of asking your friend to join the Fediverse](The%20Art%20of%20asking%20your%20friend%20to%20join%20the%20Fediverse.md)</cite>.

## Inspiration and similar practices

- Fireside Fedi, a Fediverse-adjacent podcast interviewing Fediverse personalities.
- FOSS &amp; Crafts, hosted by the co-creator of the Fediverse Christine Lemmer-Webber and her husband Morgan Lemmer-Webber.
- FediVariety, organisation, unconference and mission to involve public institutions in decentralised social networks.
