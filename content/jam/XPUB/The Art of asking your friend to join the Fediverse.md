While chatting with [Hannah](https://hannahaubry.online/ 'Hannah’s workshop') at the Mastodon booth at FOSDEM, I got an idea that brought together epiphanies and reflections I had in the past months, as I have started developing the feeling of wanting to go beyond <cite>[Knitting Our Internet](https://ournet.rocks/)</cite>.

Inspired by George Perec’s <cite>[The Art of Asking Your Boss for a Raise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Art_of_Asking_Your_Boss_for_a_Raise)</cite>, my idea is to make an interactive flowchart of a pseudo-ironic journey while introducing the Fediverse to another person.

## Why make it?

To invite a person to join the Fediverse, or even to just introduce them to the concept of decentralised social networks, is a common challenge of all decentralisation enthusiasts. As there is no <q>standard</q> or universally effective way to carry out this information process, this challenge has become sort of a meme/inside joke in the Fediphiles.

Both to make irony of this and to actually *map* all the possible <q>user journeys</q> of Fediverse onboarding, this project intends to be a diagram exploring all possible pathways to convince a clueless and potentially uninterested person to join the Fediverse.

## Workflow

Before actually having a prototype of the flowchart, I need <q>paths</q>/<q>user journeys</q> to populate it. This could be done in multiple, non-mutually exclusive ways.

- Conduct semi-structured interviews
- Ask people to respond to an online form asking them to reply to questions about mainstream social media and decentralised alternatives.
- Document the outcomes of the <cite>[Fediobsessed](Fediobsessed.md)</cite> radio show.

Once there are information and multiple options, the flowchart can be improved and enriched by simply asking the interviewee to go through it live, and successively fill the gaps with choices that were missing but that would have fit the situation.

## Prototype

The flowchart should be converted to a static, interactive website, where the diagram unfolds as the user clicks on multiple options
