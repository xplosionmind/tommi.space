While I was at [Public Spaces Conference 2026](https://conference.publicspaces.net/en) I had a lovely rabbit-hole conversation with [Marit Hoefsloot](https://marithoefsloot.com/), who made me discover <cite>[Legarsi alla Montagna](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legarsi_alla_montagna 'Legarsi alla Montagna su Wikipedia')</cite> an astounding collective art installation Sardinian artist [Maria Lai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Lai 'Maria Lai on Wikipedia') did in her home village of Ulassai, in 1981.

During the summer, while in my hometown of [Riva Ligure](#Riva%20Ligure), I have come to thinking that doing an installation like this there would be insane (almost unthinkable yet revolutionary and revelatory).

Merging this with my own practice of using the materiality of technology to convey and facilitate social relationships, I thought: why not using an **Ethernet cable** instead of a simple ribbon?

On top of the symbolic role that the connections among buildings have, there can be a more concrete, practical, technical layer: bringing Internet connection.
