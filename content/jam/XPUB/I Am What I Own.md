<q>I am what I own</q> is a reference from [Rent](Rent.md), and it enshrines the paradoxical feeling of being determined by ownership. Capitalist society requires us to estimate and give value in a strict relationship to ownership.

Is belonging the complementary inverse of ownership? Can I belong somewhere where I am not owned? Can I own something that does not belong to me?

Who owns this chirping in the background,  
to the boat or the canal, to whom does this lapping belong?  
Is my scream mine, or it becomes everyone’s as it leaves my mouth?

These questions brought me to write the riddle <cite>[What is mine](https://tommi.space/mine/)</cite>.
