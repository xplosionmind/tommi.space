## Required documents

All information in [the dedicated page](https://www.duo.nl/particulier/student-finance-citizens-eu-eer-switzerland-or-uk/supporting-documents.jsp).

<div class='red box'>

It took **eleven weeks** to get my student funding approved for the first trimester, so I basically got student funding for the first quarter of the year towards the end of that quarter.

</div>

## Supplementary grant related to parents’ income

I requested an additional grant, related to my parents’ income. To get it, I had to **submit both their tax returns** referring to two years before the one when the request was made.

After one month since my DUO funding approval, both my parents got a letter in Dutch. I translated it, but I didn’t fully understand it. Basically, it asked me to provide some information and/or documents from their side. To be sure, I made them sign a document that states that I am entitled to provide information and to take actions on their behalf.

I found all modules and some information in [this page](https://duo.nl/particulier/supplementary-grant/your-parents-income.jsp#income-outside-of-the-netherlands 'Supplementary grant').

I called DUO support because I was confused on what documents were required. It is useful to know that the funding request and the parents’ financial information are **processed by two different departments**, so there might be time discrepancies.

## Free travel

By applying to the [student travel product](https://duo.nl/particulier/student-finance/student-finance-student-travel-product.jsp) you can travel for free, *but* since the approval time is insanely long, it is likely that confirmation is received just a few days before the end of the quarter.

The only way to travel for free is to *accumulate* fines for continuing to travel as a student but without the DUO approval, and then get those fines annulled *if* your travel product is approved towards the end of the quarter.
