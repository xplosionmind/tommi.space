What is mine makes me  
What makes me is mine  
What is mean is a mine  
I don’t want a mine to be mine  
yet what’s mine ain’t just a shrine  
to tolerate is not fine  
yet comfort comes at a dime…  
To feel freedom get in line!

Not what’s mine makes me shine  
togetherness framed as divine  
yet ownership we shan’t decline.  
Where shall I draw the line,  
sharing lolly as I was nine?  
community might not always rhyme  
the friction and the sublime  
communication through mere sign  
shy and mute as a mime,  
just comply, just sign.
